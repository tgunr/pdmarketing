Turn this

Into this!

With PickADisk and a carousel you can turn a pile of disks into a neatly designed and organized storage system.
Supports the ZioTek DC-300 and DC-101 carousels.

  • Macintosh interface designed for Macintosh users by Macintosh developers
    • PC version available from manufactuer
    • Sort by title, slot number, date added, date checked
    • Search by title, tags, or comments
    • Search by kind of media, Music, Movies, Pictures, Data, etc.
    • Categorize your media by kind
    • Print out list of contents of each unit for manual operation
Features
Requirements
- Mac OS X 10.5
- Ziotek DC-300 or DC-101
Catalog, store and retrieve your collection of Blu-Ray, DVD, & CD disks.
